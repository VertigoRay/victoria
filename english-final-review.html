Priority Quest • English final 5/20

Royal English Final Review

Mission: review the big 4th-grade English skills from the whole year. Earn 120 crown XP by finishing every station.

Station 1: Grammar Guard

  • Find the subject and predicate in 5 sentences.
  • Circle nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs.
  • Fix punctuation, capitalization, and commas.
  • Practice tricky homophones: there/their/they're, to/too/two, its/it's.

Reward: +25 XP

Station 2: Reading Detective

  • Read one short passage.
  • Write the main idea in one sentence.
  • List 3 text clues that prove your answer.
  • Name the character trait or author's purpose.

Reward: +25 XP

Station 3: Writing Wizard

  • Write one strong topic sentence.
  • Add 3 supporting details.
  • Use transition words: first, next, because, finally.
  • Revise one sentence to make it more vivid.

Reward: +30 XP

Station 4: Poetry Power-Up

  • Review rhyme scheme, imagery, stanza, line, and rhythm.
  • Explain one metaphor or simile.
  • Play Poetry Quest for at least 10 minutes.

Reward: +40 XP

Parent Check

After practice, explain out loud: one grammar rule, one reading strategy, and one writing improvement. If you can teach it, you know it.
