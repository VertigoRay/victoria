Station 1: Grammar Guard

1. yesterday victoria and phillip walked to the library

2. Their going to read two poems after dinner.

3. The brave knight quickly, opened the purple door.

Teach it back: Explain one capitalization or punctuation rule you used.

Reward: +25 XP

Station 2: Reading Detective

Mia wanted to win the class garden contest, but her first seeds did not sprout. Instead of quitting, she asked her teacher what plants need. She moved the pot closer to sunlight, watered it carefully, and wrote notes each day. Two weeks later, a tiny green stem appeared. Mia smiled because her careful changes had helped the plant grow.

Main idea: What is this passage mostly about?

Text clues: Write 2 clues that prove your answer.

Character trait: Is Mia curious, careless, or impatient? Why?

Teach it back: Say the reading strategy you used: "I know because the text says..."

Reward: +25 XP

Station 3: Writing Wizard

Prompt: What is one skill you improved this school year?

Topic sentence: This year, I improved at .

Details: Add 3 reasons or examples.

Transitions: Use first, next, because, and finally.

Teach it back: Tell a parent what you changed and why it made the writing better.

Reward: +30 XP

Station 4: Poetry Power-Up

Line: "The moon was a silver coin in the sky." Is this a simile or metaphor?

Line: "The leaves danced in the wind." What picture do you see in your mind?

Vocabulary: What is a stanza? What is imagery?
