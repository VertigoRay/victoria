grammar glow

Grammar Glow-Up

Power Note

FANBOYS fix: if two complete sentences join with for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so, they usually need a comma.

Upgrade trick: swap a plain verb like went for a vivid verb like raced or tiptoed.

detective hat

Reading Detective

Power Note

Main idea: what the whole section is mostly about.

Proof: quote or point to one clue from the text that supports your answer.

math sparkle

Math Mission

Power Note

Estimate first: make a quick guess before solving.

Fraction shortcut: if denominators match, the bigger numerator makes the bigger fraction.

media radar

Media Literacy Radar

Power Note

Ask three fast questions: Who made it? What do they want me to think or do? What proof did they give?

Final Boss: Teach It Back
